AIGP‑OS

AI governance at operating system level

AIGP‑OS is an operating‑system‑level protocol designed to govern the use of Artificial Intelligence as a system capability.

Instead of treating AI as an application feature or a library, AIGP‑OS introduces an ex‑ante, non‑bypassable authorization layer that decides whether AI may be invoked before execution.

Why

Current AI governance relies largely on policies, documentation, and post‑hoc controls. AIGP‑OS addresses the missing layer: technical enforcement at operating system level.

Principles

  • Ex‑ante authorization
  • Non‑bypassable enforcement
  • Decision‑level audit by construction
  • Model‑, vendor‑ and application‑agnostic

Status

AIGP‑OS is currently defined as a protocol and architectural pattern. An enterprise reference implementation is under development.
